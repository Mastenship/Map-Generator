=== SECTION: DISCIPLINE_APPLICATION_PROTOCOL ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Apply each listed discipline to the actual building or world described by the current scratchpad. Do not merely restate a theory's name or populate generic examples. Choose world-specific consequences justified by the inputs and the setting. Distinguish physical fact, authoring assumption, approximate inference and unresolved detail. Preserve the DM's explicit constraints over generated defaults.

[Application_Requirements]
Scientific_Foundation:
A body of scientific, engineering or interpretive knowledge that helps explain mechanisms and constraints relevant to the stage.
Reasoning_Questions:
Concrete issues the model must settle by connecting its disciplinary foundations with actual earlier layer results.
Scratchpad_Consequences:
The domain and physical facts the stage owns and writes for later stages to consume.
Cross_Domain_Boundaries:
Distinguish facts belonging to this COT from those another stage will author or refine.
Model_Limitations:
- A conceptual COT is not a licensed structural calculation, electrical safety design, infectious-disease model or high-fidelity fluid dynamics simulator.
- When geometry, distance, unit conversion, flow capacity or other precise computations materially affect correctness, write measurable parameters or a pending calculation rather than presenting false numerical precision.
- A scientific framework may be applicable only to certain technologies, climates, cultures or building functions; do not apply it as an unconditional universal rule.
- The world may be magical, extraterrestrial or counterfactual. Establish the DM's physical departures first, then reason consistently within those departures.
- Materials, social practices, historical interpretations and personal significance are not deducible from physical climate alone.
- No model may infer a feature is absent solely because it is not mentioned by another scratchpad stage.
- Environmental descriptions are DM-world facts; character perceptions, inferences and secret knowledge are selected later by the parser.
Scratchpad_Continuity:
- Read the current relevant scratchpad layers before each COT.
- Reuse previous decisions, objects, site history, causal conditions, assumptions and exact spatial references.
- Do not repeatedly regenerate the entire world from scratch.
- Immediately write each completed COT's Output_Data into its designated section, then show the DM what changed.
- On revision, retain DM corrections unless the DM explicitly changes them. Resolve affected downstream dependencies as needed.
Attention_And_Context:
The scratchpad is durable shared authoring memory, not proof that an LLM has an unlimited context window. Read relevant sections, summaries and linked definitions, while keeping the full stored record available to final synthesis.
Decision_Types:
- Established by DM or inherited authoritative map.
- Generated consequence supported by the stage's applied foundations.
- Working approximation requiring later refinement.
- Unspecified or DM-reserved.
Do_Not_Require:
Do not mandate every sensory modality, dramatic event, object, person, hidden clue or scientific measurement in every room. Require adequate causal specificity for the actual place being authored.

=== SECTION: MULTISCALE_SCIENTIFIC_APPLICATION ===

[Scale_And_Inheritance]
Foundations:
Cartography; geographic information science; scale-dependent geomorphology, ecology and human geography; cultural diffusion; urban morphology.
World_Continuity:
Each completed map preserves its asset library and established geographic placements. When authoring a more detailed map, inherit relevant source assets and spatial facts before applying the COTs.
Country_To_Valley:
A country may establish the river, watershed and settlements. Valley COTs preserve their identities while developing smaller tributaries, banks, farms and local habitats.
Boundary_Transitions:
When source landscapes or neighborhoods meet, apply physical, ecological, social and historical explanations. Different materials or cultural influences may blend, coexist or remain sharply separate.
Asset_Adaptation:
The child map creates its own asset library with locally adapted assets and source references; unique source-specific positions and histories are not blindly transferred.
Reasoning_Questions:
- Which source facts are established and must remain consistent?
- Which details are genuinely unresolved at this map scale?
- What causal mechanisms explain transitions and local variation?
Status:
Working scientific guidance. See the updated outdoor and indoor pass-through drafts for full responsibilities.

=== SECTION: OUTDOOR_01_PHYSICS ===

[Scientific_Foundations]
Planetary_Formation_Theory:
Accretion, internal differentiation, rotation and orbital conditions when the world is planetary.
Gravitational_Physics:
How gravity and planetary scale affect weight, erosion, construction, fluid movement and atmosphere retention.
Atmospheric_Evolution:
Atmospheric sources, composition, pressure, planetary history and plausible long-term change.
Energy_Balance_And_Climate_Forcing:
Energy received, reflected, stored and reradiated; roles of illumination, albedo and orbital configuration.
Hydrological_Cycle:
Evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff, infiltration and storage, for the defined liquid and setting.

[Reasoning_Questions]
- What physical laws and environmental parameters does the DM intend to resemble or depart from?
- What gravity, atmosphere, illumination, surface liquids and geological timescales constrain later geography?
- Which physical parameters are globally stable and which depend on region or epoch?
- If the setting is nonplanetary or magical, which models are inapplicable or modified?

[Scratchpad_Consequences]
Writes_To:
GENERATED_PHYSICS
Output_Responsibilities:
Define consistent global premises and known deviations, with approximate or exact values only where supported. Later outdoor and indoor stages inherit these conditions but need not rerun planetary formation.
Source_Lineage:
Retains the original COT_PHYSICS interest in planetary formation, atmospheres, gravity and hydrology. Adds explicit treatment of model applicability.
