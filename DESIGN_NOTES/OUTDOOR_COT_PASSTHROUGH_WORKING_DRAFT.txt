=== SECTION: AUTHORING_WORKFLOW ===

[[LLM_INSTRUCTION]]: Execute outdoor world-building stages as successive contributions to a single evolving, DM-editable scratchpad. Each COT reads the currently relevant scratchpad layers, makes domain-grounded decisions, immediately writes its Output_Data to its designated scratchpad section and presents that update to the DM for review and corrections. A COT may be revisited when a later fact creates a real dependency conflict. Do not introduce ChaOS-like proposal/commit machinery into this collaborative map-authoring process.

[Core_Principles]
World_Truth:
The map may contain more geography, history, ecology, material detail and sensory information than current ChaOS needs. The future parser selects what a character can learn.
Physical_Causality:
Each stage applies relevant sciences to the inherited facts rather than providing unrelated decorative geography.
Spatial_Continuity:
A forest, river, watershed, road or mountain range must remain physically coherent through neighboring regions and nested map scales.
Human_Causality:
Settlement and cultural practices result from institutions, trade, migration, technologies, historical events, choices and environmental constraints; climate does not uniquely determine culture.
History:
A normal farming valley may show routine land use and generations of repairs without requiring catastrophe. A devastated region may show causally consistent evidence of the event.
Precision:
Do not fabricate high-precision measurements, engineering safety certifications or environmental model outputs. Specify known facts, plausible grounded approximations and unknowns separately.
DM_Role:
User-supplied constraints and corrections persist; a rerun must use the corrected active scratchpad, not silently reintroduce earlier generated choices.
Rendering:
Exact geographic facts are separate from the map grid, which is a presentation/indexing choice. One world record may be displayed with hexes, measured-square gridlines, or no grid.
Context:
The scratchpad stores cumulative results; the LLM still has finite context. Read pertinent saved layers and stable references rather than assuming everything fits in an active prompt.

=== SECTION: COORDINATE_AND_RENDERING_DECISION ===

[Open_Design_Decision]
Question:
Should the outdoor map's canonical geometry be tied directly to a hex lattice, or use measured geographic coordinates with optional hex indexing?
Current_Decision:
OPEN
Recommended_Direction:
Represent actual positions and shapes in a shared measured coordinate space; derive display hexes, square coordinates and other overlays as alternative views.
Status:
RECOMMENDATION_NOT_RATIFIED

[Geographic_Geometry_Proposal]
World_Reference:
- At world or continental scope, define an appropriate global frame or a documented fictional-world approximation.
- At a local or regional scope, use a consistent east/north measured plane with explicitly stated origin, axes, units, scale and parent transform.
- Connect adjacent map tiles and parent/child maps using explicit placement, resolution and coordinate transformations.
Feature_Geometries:
- Points for specific sites, springs, buildings or landmark centers when appropriate.
- Continuous lines or curves for streams, rivers, roads, borders, faults, ridges, coastlines and other elongated structures.
- Polygons for lakes, climate zones, soil units, biomes, settlements, fields and irregular territories.
- Elevation samples, contours or fields for topography, with resolution suitable to the current map scale.
- Area and local-feature definitions with stable IDs, physical properties and submap references.
Hex_View:
- Derive hex index and adjacency for movement, exploration, regional encounter design or DM navigation.
- Preserve existing GRID_TOOLS/HEX/Hex_Grid_With_Edge_Midpoints.py as a useful drawing reference, not the required physical geometry.
- A river crossing a hex should not be forced to pass through edge-midpoint display anchors.
Measured_Square_View:
- Use simple eastings and northings for precise referencing or military-style readouts.
- The real-world UTM and MGRS standards are Earth-specific implementations; borrow their practical measured-grid/reference concepts without claiming a fictional world obeys their global zones.
- The existing square-grid tool provides a graphical starting point, not a completed world projection.
Unresolved_Details:
- Absolute world geodesy or projection choice; local-versus-global transforms; axis directions; units and orientation.
- Fixed cell width for any particular hex overlay and whether multiple zoom resolutions are supported.
- Cell indexing/offset and hex orientation on the final presentation.
- Renderer API for drawing vector features and compositing transparent overlays.
- Spatial precision appropriate to a continental map versus a street or room map.
Scale_Principle:
Do not represent every visible blade of grass, chair or odor plume on a mile-scale regional map. Give regional phenomena appropriate resolution, and use linked local/site/indoor maps for finer detail without losing physical parent-location ties.

- Is this a new world, a neighboring region, or a local area inside a known map?
- Which orientation, units, map bounds, resolutions and inherited facts are authoritative?

- Which actual coordinate conventions remain unresolved but need an explicit placeholder?

OUTDOOR_GENERATED_SCOPE_AND_REFERENCE_FRAME

Map extent, origin/axes/units or provisional geometry convention, reference scale, neighboring boundaries, parent/child identifiers, constraints and expected output resolution.
Ownership_Limit:
This COT does not create mountains, forests or settlements. It defines how their physical locations and relationships can be preserved and later drawn.

=== SECTION: SMALL_PROTOTYPE_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS ===

[Suggested_Proof_Of_Concept]
Terrain:
Generate a coherent small watershed with ridge, valley, stream, riparian corridor, hill slopes, and soil variation.
Ecology:
Place a natural transition between two habitats without making it a sharp display-cell border.
Human:
Add a settlement or farm, at least two differently motivated travel routes, and one ordinary land-use trace.
Rendering:
Draw the same river, road, ridge, settlement and ecological zone with a hex overlay, measured-square overlay and no grid, ensuring the geometry does not change.
Parent_Child:
Attach one local or indoor submap to a site on the regional map and verify orientation/position.
Evaluation:
Use measured continuity and DM readability to judge whether the chosen world geometry survives all view styles.
Historical_Benchmark:
Black Mesa is an indoor reference for emergent human traces; use similarly ordinary historical traces outdoors, not a forced disaster.
