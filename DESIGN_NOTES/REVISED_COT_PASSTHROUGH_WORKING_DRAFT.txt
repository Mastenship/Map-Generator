Purpose:

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: DESIGN_FOUNDATIONS ===

[Established_Requirements]
Map_Type:
Static world or building authored before play; indoor maps may originate standalone or from an outdoor parent map.
Core_Workflow:
- DM supplies requirements and optional parent-map context.
- A COT reads the relevant current scratchpad sections and domain foundations.
- That COT immediately writes its output to the corresponding scratchpad section.
- The DM receives a copy of the added or changed content and can correct it directly.
- Subsequent COTs use the scratchpad after those corrections.
- Once the required layers are populated and reconciled, the LLM assembles the final map.
No_Precommit_Gate:
Do not substitute ChaOS's governance, proposal registry, or single-writer procedure for this DM-stepped authoring workflow.
Output_Use:
Preserve a complete underlying static world description; produce an intelligible map for a DM and future parser-facing map data without confusing those audiences.
Formatting:
Use the original LLM_FORMATTING_GUIDELINES_TEMPLATE: section markers, section breaks, instruction markers, labeled fields, contiguous hyphenated lists, and isolated special markers. Do not replace the COT language with YAML, JSON, or pipe tables.
Narrative_Depth:
A location's construction, history, occupation, ordinary routines, significant events if any, and accumulated evidence should inform physical details. Do not mandate a plot, tragedy, mystery, or person-specific sentimental item for each place.
Perception_Boundary:
The generator writes world facts and starting conditions, not character-specific knowledge. A future parser delivers perceptible and known slices according to character location, abilities, gear, barriers, and authorized in-play changes.

=== SECTION_BREAK ===

=== SECTION: GENERAL_PASS_THROUGH_RULES ===

[Pass_Through_Contract]
Inputs:
- Consult the current active scratchpad, not merely the previous COT's isolated response.
- Read the specific earlier sections required by the current responsibility.
- Preserve relevant user-authored constraints, DM corrections, and accepted world facts.
- Treat imported outdoor context as optional for standalone indoor generation.
Outputs:
- Record a conceptually meaningful explanation and the physical or spatial facts owned by that stage.
- Give each significant physical feature a stable reference when later layers must address, move, modify, render, hide, inspect, or connect to it.
- Record exact locations, spatial extents, elevation, dimensions, and relationships whenever a decision is spatial and renderable.
- Refer to an existing object, wall, opening, zone, or asset definition by its established identity instead of independently reinventing it.
- Mark genuinely unspecified detail as unspecified; do not equate omitted data with a positive assertion that the feature is absent.
Update:
- Write the completed Output_Data to the assigned active scratchpad section immediately.
- Append the actual execution and update to GENERATION_LOG after each pass. Do not pre-populate the log with invented execution timestamps.
- Supply the DM the corresponding newly written or changed information.
- The DM may directly revise the scratchpad before the next stage.
- On a rerun, use current DM-corrected content as input and identify replaced or dependent facts; do not silently restore earlier generated values.
Dependency_Handling:
- If later work makes an earlier placement impossible, revise the relevant earlier section in cooperation with the DM and update dependent sections.
- Check relevant physical contradictions as needed and at final assembly; do not impose a ChaOS-like governance cycle.
Early_Requirements:
- Distinguish rough requirements used before construction from exact later placements. Preliminary requirements may be refined rather than copied as final geometry.
Spatial_Truth:
- Keep shared wall, door, and opening identities canonical; do not permit two neighboring cells to independently disagree about the same physical boundary.
- Keep world asset definitions, specific placed objects, and visual drawing stamps separate.
Rendering:
- A reusable deterministic renderer may create DM-facing SVG overlays on grid paper from the authored spatial description. Rendering never independently invents or authorizes world facts.
- Early drawings are optional previews of completed layers; the final assembled map remains the publication step.

- 08 COT_INFRASTRUCTURE_LOGIC: roads, bridges and communications links consistent with settlements and terrain; writes GENERATED_INFRASTRUCTURE.
- 09 COT_LANDMARK_POPULATOR: historically and geographically grounded points of interest; writes GENERATED_LANDMARKS.
- 10 FINAL_MAP_DATA: assemble the outdoor map from the corrected scratchpad, not from ad hoc redrawing.

[Source_Defects_And_Revisions]
Biome_Dependency:
Source asset library needs Biome_Data even though COT_BIOME_GENERATOR follows it. Correct by placing biome generation first.
Infrastructure_Format:
The historical COT_INFRASTRUCTURE_LOGIC section header is combined with instruction text on the same line. Split into conforming marker and instruction lines in any revised copy.
Physical_Path_Rules:
The old hydrology rule that a river cannot cross a square center and cannot turn 90 degrees within a square is an authoring convention, not an inherent geographical law. Reconsider it when deciding continuous-vector versus cell/edge geometry.
Cultural_Causation:
Do not force environmental determinism; cultural materials and practices may be influenced by migration, exchange, institutions, deliberate design and local adaptation as well as terrain and climate.
Outdoor_Status:
The sequence correction is the presently recommended minimum change. An exhaustive redesign of outdoor COT responsibilities and coordinates remains open.

=== SECTION: FINAL_INDOOR_MAP_ASSEMBLY ===

[Stage_Contract]
COT_Name:
COT_INDOOR_FINAL_MAP_ASSEMBLY
Owns:
Synthesis of the current, DM-corrected scratchpad into one consistent, complete static map package.
Reads:
All active indoor scratchpad sections and applicable imported outdoor or asset-library records.
Writes_To:
INDOOR_FINAL_MAP_DATA
Passes_Forward:
- One identified map, defined orientation and scale, level/elevation relationships and exact physical placement.
- Canonical named rooms, shared boundaries, door/opening references, navigable transitions and unique object identities.
- Defaults plus precise local overrides for spatial, material, hazard, object and environmental data.
- Relevant historical, personal, cultural and sensory facts retained as DM/world data even when hidden from characters.

- Compare wall/opening references, room boundaries, routes, level connections, clearances, asset instances, environmental source dependencies and corrections.
- Bring irreconcilable or material ambiguity to the DM instead of silently inventing mutually inconsistent geometry.

Finalization:
Only publish final output after the required layers have been populated and the DM has the opportunity to make corrections. This is not a proposal-commit gateway for the intermediate COT stages.
Scope:
The in-play parser, runtime world-state overlay, game adjudication, character memory/alterations and eventual separate WorldState remain distinct external responsibilities.
